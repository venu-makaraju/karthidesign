```
✅ Semantic HTML (accessibility)

✅ Hover micro-interactions (lift, shift, highlight)
✅ Professional completeness (nothing missing)
```

## 🎓 Design Philosophy

```
PREMIUM ≠ FLASHY

Premium means:
  Refined    →  Not trendy
  Confident  →  Not aggressive
  Complete   →  Not excessive
  Subtle     →  Not loud
  Timeless   →  Not dated
```
